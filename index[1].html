AI-powered translation into the direct, affectionate, low-pressure texting voice from your reference.
OpenRouter + free model
Security: Your OpenRouter API key belongs on the backend, never in this HTML file. The included server reads it from the OPENROUTER_API_KEY environment variable.
Voice rules
Direct and conversational. Emotionally open. Affectionate without being overly poetic.
Apologetic whenever the speaker thinks they crossed a boundary.
Reassuring and low-pressure. Informal and somewhat spontaneous.
Occasionally awkward or grammatically incomplete in a natural, human way.
Short sentences, fragments, simple clauses, line breaks and natural repetition.
Prefer ordinary vocabulary: cool, absolutely, very, just, feel free, whenever, ready,
talk, text, respond, sorry, apologize, no worries, special, brighter, love, miss,
catch up, tell me, until then, pard, bf, set, studio, footage.
Preserve meaning above all else. Do not add emotional intensity, affection, romance,
or apology that isn't present. Do not polish the result into formal prose.
For production deployment, host the backend over HTTPS and restrict CORS to your site.
